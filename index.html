01  The problem

Today's tools look backward. Buyers need to know what happens next.

Predicting the past is easy. You already know the answer. Predicting the future, and being graded on it, is the thing everyone else in this category avoids.

  • Surveys and focus groups are slow and expensive. A big wealth study takes about six months.
  • People do not lie to surveys on purpose, but what they say they will do and what they actually do are different things.
  • AI simulations promise to fix this, but nobody proves they are right about the future. They only reproduce the past.

05  The differentiator

The question a survey cannot answer: what happens if you act?

This is our second engine, and nobody else has it. A poll tells you where things stand today. It cannot tell you what happens if you run an ad, or if wildfires take over the news. Ours can, because it models the other side reacting, not just your first move.

The other side fights back

We model the counter-punch

A simple model assumes your ad lands once and sticks. Reality: the other side counter-mobilizes and the effect fades. Our evolving panel captures that push-back. A static model literally cannot.

We tried hard to break it

Less than 0.02% chance it is noise

We ran the statisticians' stress tests, permutation, bootstrap, jackknife, trimming, and could not make the effect disappear. Proven in testing. The out-of-sample field seal on a live race is our next milestone, and we say so plainly.
